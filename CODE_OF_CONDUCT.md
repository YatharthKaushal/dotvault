# Code of Conduct

Help keep the project welcoming and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, not people.
- Provide helpful feedback.
- Keep discussions professional.
- Respect differing opinions and technical approaches.
- Assume good intent from contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or any form of abusive behavior
will not be tolerated. This includes:

- Insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code review — and to public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **[theyatharthk@gmail.com](mailto:theyatharthk@gmail.com)**.
Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate.
